Manage Appliance Repair & Service Online

The “Appliance Repairing and Service Management System” is being developed for managing repairing services of home appliances such as refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, microwave ovens, and other electronic devices has increased the demand for timely repair and maintenance services. Traditionally, customers register repair requests through phone calls or manual service centers, which often leads to delays, miscommunication, and lack of transparency in the service process.

To solve these problems, the Appliance Repairing and Service Management System provides an easy-to-use online platform for customers, technicians, and administrators. Customers can quickly register complaints, check the status of their requests, schedule repair services, and give feedback. Technicians can view the complaints assigned to them, update service progress, record repair details, and manage their daily tasks. Administrators oversee the entire system by managing users, assigning technicians, monitoring ongoing repairs, and generating useful reports.

This system automates the entire repair lifecycle—from complaint registration to service completion—ensuring timely response, improved service quality, transparency, and customer satisfaction. By digitizing manual processes, the system minimizes errors, reduces operational workload, and creates a seamless communication bridge between all service stakeholders.
