About Appliances Repairing and Service Management System

Purpose

The purpose of this system is to automate and organize the appliance repairing process, reduce manual work, and improve service quality.

Features

  • Online appliance service booking
  • Customer complaint management
  • Technician assignment
  • Repair status tracking
  • Service history and reports

Benefits

  • Time-saving and efficient process
  • Reduced paperwork
  • Better customer satisfaction
  • Easy monitoring of services

Users of the System

  • Customers
  • Service Technicians
  • Service Center Admin

Conclusion

This system provides a reliable and user-friendly solution for managing appliance repair services. It ensures transparency, efficiency, and improved service management.
